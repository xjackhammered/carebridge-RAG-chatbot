# Partner Hospitals in Thailand

CareBridge has 6 partner hospitals in Thailand. Direct flights from Dhaka to Bangkok take about 3 hours.

## Chao Phraya Heart Institute — Bangkok
Specialties: adult cardiology, bypass and valve surgery, structural heart procedures. Known for minimally invasive cardiac surgery.

## Siam Oncology Centre — Bangkok
Specialties: medical oncology, radiotherapy, proton beam therapy, PET-CT imaging. Offers multidisciplinary tumour board review for complex cancers.

## Lanna International Hospital — Chiang Mai
Specialties: orthopaedics, spine surgery, joint replacement, sports medicine. Rehabilitation unit on site.

## Andaman Eye & Laser Hospital — Phuket
Specialties: ophthalmology, cataract surgery, retina treatment, laser vision correction. This is CareBridge's main option for eye problems, since there are no eye specialists among the Bangladesh partner doctors.

## Sukhothai Women & Children's Hospital — Bangkok
Specialties: IVF and fertility treatment, high-risk pregnancy, paediatrics, neonatal care.

## Rama Wellness Hospital — Bangkok
Specialties: full-body health check-ups, gastroenterology, endoscopy, liver clinic.
