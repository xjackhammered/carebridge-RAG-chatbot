# Partner Hospitals in India

CareBridge has 22 partner hospitals in India across 8 cities. India is the most common destination for patients from Bangladesh because of short travel time (Dhaka to Kolkata is about 1 hour by air) and the shared language support in Kolkata. Below are the main partner hospitals by specialty.

## Cardiac care
- Deccan Heart & Vascular Hospital, Hyderabad: bypass surgery, heart valve replacement, heart transplant programme.

## Cancer care
- Himalaya Cancer Institute, New Delhi: bone marrow transplant, robotic oncology surgery, paediatric cancer unit.

## Transplants
- Kaveri Transplant Centre, Chennai: liver and kidney transplant. Living-donor transplants require documentation of the donor relationship, which CareBridge helps to prepare.
- Konkan Kidney Institute, Mumbai: kidney transplant and dialysis.

## Neurology and neurosurgery
- Ganga Neurosciences Hospital, Kolkata: brain and spine surgery, stroke unit. Bengali-speaking staff are available.

## Orthopaedics
- Nilgiri Orthopaedic & Spine Hospital, Bangalore: joint replacement, spine surgery, sports injuries.

## Children
- Sahyadri Children's Hospital, Pune: paediatric cardiac surgery, paediatric neurology, congenital conditions.

## Eyes
- Marina Bay Eye Care, Chennai: cataract, retina and corneal transplant.
