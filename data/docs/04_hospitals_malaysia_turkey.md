# Partner Hospitals in Malaysia and Turkey

## Malaysia (4 partner hospitals)
- Orchid Medical Centre, Kuala Lumpur: oncology, cardiac surgery, and executive health screening.
- Penang Bayview Hospital, Penang: orthopaedics, joint replacement and post-operative rehabilitation.
- Johor Straits Fertility Centre, Johor Bahru: IVF and reproductive medicine.
- Selangor Neuro & Spine Centre, Shah Alam: neurosurgery and spine surgery.

Flight time from Dhaka to Kuala Lumpur is about 4 hours. Muslim-friendly facilities, including prayer rooms and halal meals, are available at all four hospitals.

## Turkey (3 partner hospitals)
- Bosphorus International Hospital, Istanbul: robotic surgery and bariatric (weight-loss) surgery.
- Anatolia Transplant Hospital, Ankara: bone marrow and stem cell transplant.
- Aegean Rehabilitation Hospital, Izmir: neurological rehabilitation after stroke or spinal injury.

Flights from Dhaka to Istanbul take about 8 hours. Because of the distance, Turkey is usually recommended only for treatments not available in nearer partner countries.
