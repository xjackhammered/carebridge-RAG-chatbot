# Travel Support for Overseas Patients

## Medical visa
CareBridge obtains an invitation letter from the partner hospital, which is needed for a medical visa. The letter usually takes 2 to 3 working days after the hospital accepts your case. The visa itself is issued by the embassy or visa centre, and its processing time is not controlled by CareBridge. Typical visa processing is 3 to 10 working days depending on the country.

## Airport reception
A CareBridge representative or the hospital's patient coordinator meets you at the destination airport and takes you to the hospital or accommodation.

## Interpreters
Interpreters are available for Bangla-English in Thailand and Malaysia, and for Bangla-Turkish in Turkey. In Kolkata, India, many hospital staff speak Bengali directly.

## Accommodation
CareBridge can book serviced apartments or hotels near the hospital for the patient and one or two attendants. Accommodation is paid by the patient.

## Attendants
Most partner hospitals allow one or two attendants per patient. Visa letters can include the attendants' names.

## Final reminder
Always reconfirm visa and entry rules with the destination country's embassy, since requirements change.
