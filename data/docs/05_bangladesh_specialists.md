## Specialties not currently available in Bangladesh
CareBridge currently has no partner ophthalmologists (eye specialists) and no pulmonologists (lung specialists) in Bangladesh. Patients needing these should consider the overseas options, such as Andaman Eye & Laser Hospital in Phuket, Thailand, or call CareBridge for advice.
